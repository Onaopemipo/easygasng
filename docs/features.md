# Feature inventory

Use [the current feature verification matrix](reports/easygas-feature-verification.md) for every baseline feature plus the product/service expansion, its source, tests and remaining work. There are 55 baseline groups and 22 new verification groups; these are review groupings, not a completion score.

Implemented local capabilities span customer shopping/accounts, branch operations, POS, serialised empty-cylinder allocation, independently governed catalogue/pricing, customer service appointments, qualified staff/slots, quote approval, material reconciliation, private photographs, immutable reports/customer acknowledgement, restricted credit/cash/refund controls, bundle checkout, compatibility evidence, assessment recommendations, follow-ups, reconciliation, policies and operational incidents.

Web/native share the same persisted APIs. New native onboarding/report/share/media/offline draft code compiles and exports; physical-device acceptance is outstanding. Real stock, service availability, credentials, safety checklists and published business policies were not fabricated. External payments and messages remain unconnected, and no production deployment has occurred.

The larger master scope remains incomplete in the specific areas listed in [known limitations](known-limitations.md) and the [outstanding work register](reports/easygas-outstanding-work.md). Earlier audit artifacts are preserved under `docs/audits/`.
