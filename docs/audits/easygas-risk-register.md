## Post-remediation residual targets and dependencies

These are **targets**, not claimed completed risk reductions. R01 target <=5 after independent safety/allocation/physical-procedure validation; R02–R05 <=4 after API/browser regression and managed-pg races; R06 <=6 only after hosted restore/rollback/worker monitoring drills; R07 <=4 after real sandbox delivery acceptance; R08 <=6 after legal/privacy operations signoff; R09 <=4 after compatible fixes and exploitability review; R10 <=6 after device/store readiness; R11 <=6 after measured pg stress; R12/R20 <=6 after timestamp/feedback/calibration cases; R13 <=3 after responsive regressions; R14–R16 <=6 after finance/stock reconciliation; R17 <=4 after failed-delivery/offline recovery; R18 <=6 after commercial tenancy/credit acceptance; R19 <=6 after channel recovery evidence; R21 <=6 after target-load benchmarks; R22 <=6 after abuse/security exercises; R23 <=4 after reviewed release controls; R24 <=6 after disconnected/replayed operation tests; R25 <=4 after finance-approved clawback tests.

R0 corrections precede R1 launch hardening. R18 depends on R02, R14–R16 and approved commercial policy. R19/R20 depend on R07 and messaging consent/provider decisions. Optional advanced modules depend on this register and do not supersede P0/P1 risks. Owners are recommended accountable **roles**; no person has accepted an assignment yet. Every listed effort is engineering working days for one experienced contributor plus review, not a delivery commitment; parallel work, data migration and business approval can alter the schedule.
