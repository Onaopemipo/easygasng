# Canonical data contract and money

`packages/business-rules/src/financial-document.ts` is a presentation adapter, not a new money ledger. Order/service invoice totals, identifiers and effective notes remain supplied by invoices.ts/service-invoices.ts + invoiceBalance. Issued numbers and immutable snapshot triggers are unchanged. The adapter validates safe integer kobo and bounded rows/content; money formatting uses integer division with two exact decimal places. No new tax/fee/discount is computed by a PDF.

Logical fields: type/title/number, captured business/customer identity, issued time (Africa/Lagos), due date, canonical source/payment references, correction version, actual lines and totals, authoritative status, explicit sandbox/environment, context notices. Receipt requires `verifiedPostingId`, actual wallet/payment ledger ID, exact principal+processing=gross=verified paid and zero receipt balance. Only payments/documents.ts supplies these from a committed posting. No client screenshot/redirect can create that posting.

Invoice states: unpaid, partially paid, paid, voided or reconciliation required. Overdue is a separate recorded-date indication. Even a paid invoice never displays `Payment Verified`; that badge is exclusive to a verified payment receipt. Credit/debit/void notes require an approved canonical note and reference the original number + existing sequence; they are adjustments, not refund confirmations. A requested/provider-unknown refund cannot be printed as refunded.

Receipt principal is obligation payment or wallet funding as actually recorded; fee is separate, never wallet value or additional invoice revenue. Collection success is not merchant bank settlement. Customer fee consent and approved gateway policy/gross-up/idempotency remain in the existing orchestrator; only a descriptive document snapshot was added to its immutable quote. New quotes capture business identity, customer display name/stable ID, original invoice/booking reference and template version. Later setting/customer edits do not rewrite those snapshots. Legacy receipts without identity capture omit missing optional business details, with absence recorded as API diagnostics; they are not backfilled from current settings. Optional configuration/approval diagnostics are kept out of the customer print model; the owner/finance settings screen provides the restricted preview/warning.

Original invoice items, one-visit delivery fee, approved discounts and net verified payment balance remain distinct from receipt gross. No new delivery fee on add-on notes, no withdrawal, no duplicated customer/wallet/payment model or invoice numbering sequence. Historical Excel records remain unverified unless separately reconciled; actual import is still held.
