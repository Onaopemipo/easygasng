# Products, professional services and bundles

The eight user-supplied empty-cylinder/refill records and prices are preserved. Categories now include gas gauges, lighters, hoses, valves and regulators. A category does not invent a stocked product: new products start without approved prices, stock or sale publication. Owners manage unique SKU, one-level parent variants, brand/model/specifications, warranty, installation requirement and archive state. Historical order items retain their original names and prices.

Photos accept decoded still PNG/JPEG/WebP, bounded input/decoded dimensions and per-target counts. Server processing orients, resizes without enlargement, converts to WebP and removes embedded metadata. SVG/active formats are rejected. Published product/service photos are public; booking evidence is private and becomes locked after report submission. These are persisted image bytes, not arbitrary remote URLs. Product images support ordering/archiving; a full service-image lifecycle/editor remains limited.

Specific compatibility records identify exact equipment brand, model, connection and pressure plus evidence. Proposed records are unpublished until independent review. Verified-compatible records additionally require a current safety-review qualification and disappear when it expires. Broad product metadata cannot assert blanket verified compatibility. Compatibility is never inferred from a cylinder size or a photograph.

Four service records are introduced as **drafts**: Cooker Servicing, Cooker Installation, Cylinder Installation and Gas Safety Checks. Activation needs actual business pricing/areas/duration/qualification/preparation/safety/warranty/inclusions/exclusions and an approved checklist. No real services were activated merely to fill the storefront.

Bundles are independently approved configurations of exact product quantities and one fixed-price service. Products keep their approved prices. A 0–30% configured discount applies only to the original service labour price; call-out, delivery and additional customer-approved work remain separate. Checkout revalidates bundle version and displayed subtotal, stock and appointment, then saves immutable bundle and booking price snapshots. The customer deliberately selects the bundle cart; it is never added automatically. Bundled service cannot start until linked products have been delivered. Current product fulfilment is Lagos-only; mixed bundles enforce that boundary.

Recommendation rules offer reviewed assessment/unknown-compatibility support based on current selected products or verified delivered purchase history, stock, active service and optional area. Display/select/book/complete events record actual actions with deduplication. They do not constitute automatic diagnosis or a full measured recommendation model. Scheduling remains authoritative; a suggestion is not a guaranteed slot. Bidirectional personalised service-to-product recommendation coverage is incomplete; quotations list real approved accessories and require customer approval.
