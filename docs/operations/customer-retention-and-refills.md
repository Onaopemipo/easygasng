# Customer retention and refill estimates

Existing consumption_profiles and verified delivered refill history remain per cylinder. The forecast now accepts two consistent verified purchase records and marks two/three-refill output preliminary with low confidence. It excludes unverified, other-cylinder, future and invalid interval history. It estimates purchase intervals, not measured remaining gas or actual consumption. No sensor claim or automatic campaign activation is made.

Existing feedback/preferences/quiet hours remain; all requested external-refill/irregular/partial-fill adjustments and forecast accuracy acceptance are not complete. CRM segmentation thresholds, follow-up ownership/contact history, win-back budget and real conversion reports are outstanding. SMS/WhatsApp reminders need separately approved provider, channel consent, purpose, caps and suppression; transactional consent is not marketing consent.
