# Service booking and technician operations

1. An owner/service manager proposes business configuration. Independent owner approval activates the version. A separately approved safety checklist needs an owner and a currently qualified safety reviewer.
2. Independently verify actual technician qualifications and expiry. Create explicit future service slots for a named eligible technician. Duration and qualification through the appointment end are checked; blocked or overlapping slots cannot be booked. There is no fabricated appointment availability.
3. The signed-in customer selects a service version, slot, covered address, phone, equipment details and concerns. Server validation snapshots configuration/checklist and reserves the technician transactionally. A stable customer request key permits matching retries and rejects changed contents.
4. A service manager confirms and marks the assignment. The assigned qualified technician records travel and arrival/start. Rescheduling chooses another validated slot with a current booking version; pre-work cancellation releases reservations. Slot selection may reassign the qualified technician. Public times use Africa/Lagos; explicitly labelled datetime inputs use the operator device timezone.
5. Additional work needs an itemised immutable quotation revision. Accessory prices come from approved branch inventory. The customer accepts/declines the current revision; only approval reserves materials. Issued materials prevent casually replacing scope.
6. Stores issue approved quantities and actual unit cost. Technician records used/damaged quantities; stores confirms all unused physical returns. Completion requires reconciliation. Unused units are returned once and are excluded from the invoice.
7. The assigned qualified technician submits the exact approved checklist, evidence notes, findings, work actually performed and outcome. Failed checks cannot be labelled success. Unsafe/unable/further-assessment reports fail the service and create a supervisor incident. A report is immutable and is **not regulatory certification**.
8. The customer reviews/downloads/prints the report and acknowledges a successful documented outcome before completion. Safe completion is not inferred from payment. Feedback is accepted only for an owned completed booking.
9. Document a follow-up after acknowledged completion. Customer reminder consent/quiet hours apply, notices deduplicate, dismissal is owned and rebooking links the original follow-up. No order/payment is generated by a reminder.

Web routes: `/services`, `/services/<id>`, `/bookings`, `/combined-checkout`; staff: `/admin/services`, `/admin/bookings`, `/admin/technicians`, `/admin/quotations`, `/admin/incidents`. Native implements the same customer and assigned technician workflows, image attachment and PDF/JSON report sharing. Device acceptance has not been run.

Linked checkout atomically creates product reservations and appointment requests. Any failure rolls back the entire request; both retain independent fulfilment/payment histories. Stable request fingerprints survive explicit retry. Full uncertain-network recovery UI and production PostgreSQL concurrency acceptance remain necessary.

Limitations: explicit slots rather than a complete holiday/roster calendar; no complete mid-job cancellation/dispute/quote-expiry engine; no provider deposit/refund integration; qualification evidence references do not replace real credential validation; no real field safety review has occurred.
