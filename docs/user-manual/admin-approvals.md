# Admin approvals

Admin approval is final. You do not need another Admin or Finance colleague to approve your request.

1. Open the existing setting or operation, enter the details and select its review/confirmation action.
2. Check the summary, then confirm once. If prompted, verify your own password.
3. The result is applied immediately, scheduled for the selected date, or explains the evidence/version issue that prevents activation. Audit History records the action.

For an existing pending request, open **Approval Centre**, select it and choose **Approve** once. Drafts must first be submitted. Other staff requests also complete with one Admin approval.

A staff invitation must first be accepted by its recipient. Its access plan then needs one Admin approval. An Admin can grant another existing staff member access directly through Staff Management. You cannot change your own privileges.

A request's approval does not invent actual payment receipts, LPG measurements, delivery photos, customer agreement or qualification evidence. Complete those records when the workflow asks for them. If an authorised change fails to activate, fix the displayed cause and retry it; another approver is not required.

These rules are available on the demo with migration085. The current hosted audit and any remaining release gates are tracked in the requirements matrix.

## Tested approval paths

| Area | What Admin does |
| --- | --- |
| Settings, prices, policies and checklists | Confirm the change once; future changes show Scheduled. |
| Saved drafts and staff requests | Submit a draft or choose Approve on a pending request once. A saved justification is retained; another operational note is optional. |
| Staff access | After the recipient accepts an invitation, approve its exact access plan once. Existing staff access can be changed directly. Own-privilege changes remain blocked. |
| Points, referral risk, credit and refunds | Confirm the exact customer, branch and amount or risk decision once. Audit History retains the result. |
| Inventory, purchases, transfers and expenses | Confirm the documented operation once. Receiving stock and recording payment still need actual receipts or measurements. |
| POS and collected cash | Record what was received, then reconcile it as Admin. You can reconcile your own collection; Finance approval is unnecessary. |
| Service cash | Open the booking and use **Record cash actually collected**, then verify that receipt as Admin. No technician sign-in is needed. |
| Invoice corrections, cancellations and settlements | Approve the exact correction or documented settlement once. Customer consent remains separate where required. |
| LPG and product discrepancies | A qualified Admin can request and complete the discrepancy review. Required measurements and custody reconciliation still apply. |
| Campaigns, compatibility, recommendations and testimonials | One Admin review is final. Consent, factual evidence and any separate publication action still apply. |

Re-entering your password confirms your identity; it does not ask another person to approve. Accepting an assignment, acknowledging a physical load, recording a qualification from genuine evidence and obtaining customer agreement are operational records. Admin approval does not replace them. An Admin cannot certify their own technical qualification or change their own privileges.

The hosted audit used labelled synthetic branches and accounts. Settings with external or global effects were approved as future changes and cancelled before activation. Doorstep additions remain release-disabled on the public demo; their approval handlers were checked separately in temporary QA deployments. This audit does not authorise commercial production or enable any held capability.
