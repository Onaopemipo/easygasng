# Business continuity

Application outage: platform operator records incident/time/build, checks health and database ownership, preserves diagnostics, restores a compatible application and performs read-back checks before writes resume. Database outage: fail closed; never acknowledge a booking not known committed; keep original operation identity and reconcile after connectivity returns. Retry definite deadlock/serialization aborts only; ambiguous COMMIT/network failures require read-back. Worker outage: pending durable records remain in the database; inspect job/incident state and restart the existing authorized maintenance command after checking local single-owner constraints. Do not restart the booking transaction merely to deliver a notification.

Controlled manual fallback: authorized support records a random UUID temporary reference, capture timestamp/staff, customer contact, requested service/address/time, status REQUEST ONLY and consent to contact. Use restricted encrypted business storage, never a public spreadsheet. Do not promise capacity, qualified personnel, safety clearance or payment receipt. On recovery, service manager checks existing customer bookings for duplicates and actual available qualified capacity; customer confirms a valid request through the normal ownership-protected workflow. Attach the manual reference to the support/incident record and booking instructions; close it only after a saved authoritative reference is verified. No staff impersonation, bulk manual ingestion or new override privilege was added. A full manual reconciliation drill remains outstanding.

Responsibility mapping: owner/technical operator for application or database recovery; service_manager for schedule review; inventory/owner for physical materials; finance with existing independent approval for money; qualified safety_officer for unsafe findings; customer support through existing support/incident capabilities. Database restoration remains an operator-level process, not a newly granted web role. Business owner and technical lead must approve restart after critical incidents.

RPO/RTO are not approved. Local encrypted snapshots support recovery testing but do not provide supervised/off-site production continuity. Proposed targets must be chosen after backup frequency, storage durability, staffing and measured restoration drills are agreed. Measure actual backup age and complete restore/reconciliation duration during the next authorized drill; do not advertise a numerical guarantee from build success.
