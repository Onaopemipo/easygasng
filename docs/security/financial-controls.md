# Financial controls

No new general financial ledger or payment integration is introduced. Verified payments, cash collections, POS shifts, wallet/reward entries, customer-approved credit/refund requests, service invoices and material records remain authoritative. Expense records/events document operating expenses and actual payment evidence; they never overwrite wallet balances, issue transfers or manufacture verified customer payments.

Owner and branch-scoped finance roles may read financial details/costs and generate sensitive exports. Managers retain branch operational revenue cards but cannot query detailed financial/profit/cost reports. Sales/inventory/dispatcher/technical administration see no revenue totals, liability totals, acquisition receipts or expense details through the new snapshot. Consolidated wallet/reward liabilities remain owner-only. Existing legacy purchasing acquisition costs are now redacted for operational roles.

Expense flow: draft → pending shared review → approved → recorded → paid. Rejection/cancellation/expiry is derived from the canonical approval request while the original expense/evidence survives. Amount/date/payee/category/description fingerprint and unique business reference catch duplicate submissions. A distinct owner approves; recording is separate from approval; a different finance/owner colleague verifies actual payment evidence. Active approval authority is rechecked before recording/payment. Unique event per expense/kind prevents duplicate postings. Reversal requires a new independent request, appends an event and preserves the original recorded/paid events. No destructive completed-record endpoint exists.

Revenue is verified completed order receipts less approved order refunds, independently verified POS cash-up, and completed service invoice receipts net of approved service refunds. Delivery fee is read once from each historical order. Total cash waiting for verification remains distinct. Service/order/POS components remain separate to avoid double-counting mixed bookings. Gross product-line figures do not allocate unclassified partial refunds to categories; net order refund deductions are shown separately. Current product categories may differ from historical category labels, which were not originally snapshotted.

Reports state metric provenance: authoritative recorded figures; calculated from complete recorded inputs; unavailable missing costs/definitions. No definitive profit or inventory value from selling prices. Weighted average is available only with an approved method and complete cost movements; incomplete COGS/overhead prevents net/gross profit. Unconnected online payments, withdrawals and customer transfers remain unchanged. Purchase approval/receipt is not supplier-payment verification; supplier payables require actual reconciliation inputs and currently remain unverified.

Generation and scheduled-snapshot downloads recheck current scope/permissions and audit the action. Exports reuse identical returned rows: CSV, Excel-compatible SpreadsheetML XML, and browser print/save-to-PDF. No native XLSX or server-rendered PDF is claimed. Stored scheduled snapshots are private and accessible only by their owning user with current report authority. External delivery is unconnected; only persisted in-app notifications are reported as available.

Use existing finance reconciliation/refund workflows for corrections. Do not edit completed payments, original invoices or wallet balances. No legal/accounting completeness certification is implied.
