# Administrator guide: gas pricing and delivery

Open **Admin → Settings**. The Gas pricing & delivery cards show the current unit gas price and flat delivery fee. **Update or schedule** opens the same proposal form used by other settings. Enter the proposed amount in naira, a reason, and optionally an effective date in Africa/Lagos. Choose Save as draft to prepare it without submission. Otherwise submit it for independent owner review. Existing review policy retains its six-day scheduling horizon and seven-day approval window.

Use **Approval Centre** to edit a draft, submit it, review old/proposed values, request changes, reject, withdraw, review partial approvals or retry a failed activation after correcting the cause. Own restricted requests cannot be approved. Sensitive approvers verify their identity. Future changes remain scheduled until due and revalidation succeeds. History and rollback remain in each setting's history dialog; reversal creates a fresh approved version.

Choose **Gas Pricing → Gas quantity and cylinder sizes** to configure the minimum, maximum, increment, decimal precision and active capacities. Required limits start unconfigured. Never enter arbitrary operating limits simply to enable checkout. Choose **Delivery Locations**, select a location, and propose its name, status, visibility, order, description, coverage policy and coverage handling. Archive requires inactive and hidden flags. The broader management disclosure provides Add delivery location and real order/delivery counts.

Authorised inventory staff can receive documented bulk gas in grams, inspect pending gas orders, register a new customer cylinder intake in quarantine, and record a verified additional fill allowance. Complete the documented cylinder inspection in Cylinders before filling eligibility is accepted. On an order, authorised operations staff use **Confirm actual address coverage** with evidence. An owner must review withdrawn-location fulfilment. Orders cannot dispatch without applicable evidence and eligible cylinders.

The report panel supports dates, branch, location, status, payment status, cylinder capacity, quantity range and customer category, with CSV, Excel XML and Print/Save PDF. Review only genuine receipts and stock. Quantity limits, boundaries, stock, qualified operations, legal acceptance and payment arrangements still require business input.
