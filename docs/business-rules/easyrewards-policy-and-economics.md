# EasyRewards policy and economics

No actual EasyRewards economics were approved in this phase. The prompt's example rates remain examples. Seeded policy has earning/redemption/referrals OFF, zero referral bonus. Both technical flags default OFF. Local synthetic test approvals are isolated and do not constitute business approval.

`loyalty.policy` uses the existing settings/change request/version engine: risk 4, additional password verification, independent owner AND finance approvals, base-version conflict detection, effective dates, immutable setting_versions and audit. The requester cannot approve themselves. Runtime requires a matching active, activated version/value; directly flipping old loyalty.enabled is insufficient. Current policy disabling earning pauses queued jobs, while their original economics stay frozen for an approved resumption.

Policy schema: spendPerPointKobo, discountPerPointKobo, minimumPoints, maximumDiscountBps, explicit eligibleCategories, earningEnabled, redemptionEnabled, referralEnabled/referralPoints. Integer kobo and BigInt arithmetic, floor rounding. Eligible line amounts exclude delivery; reward discounts are subtracted before earning. No services, tiers, welcome bonus, automatic expiry or gamification. Product tax separation is not modelled; do not activate for tax-inclusive eligibility without an approved tax allocation change. Partial fulfilment/refunds have no authoritative lifecycle here and remain blocked for activation.

Checkout discounts require the independently approved policy AND EASYGAS_REWARDS_REDEMPTION_ENABLED=true. Server rechecks available points, minimum, eligible lines and cap; unchanged configured delivery fee is paid monetarily. No cash-out or point-to-wallet transfer. Discounts commit with checkout; cancellation or independently verified full refund restores spent points exactly once. Full cash/wallet refund uses the actual reduced order total, never the gross product price.

Full refund reverses original earned/referral points immutably. Insufficient remaining points restrict spending and create finance review; no automatic wallet debit. `loyalty.adjustment` requires independent owner and finance approval, a real scoped customer, nonzero points, reason and evidence. This is a correction, not an unapproved earning offer.

Referral bonus is only on the first paid verified fulfilled purchase created after attribution, with positive eligible spend and active same-branch referrer. Signup, repeat payment, refunded prior first purchase and later orders do not qualify. Codes are random and public share URLs require configured HTTPS APP_URL; local development does not emit public share links. Shared addresses are not classified as fraud. Suspicious-pattern review tooling, campaign budgets and qualified business acceptance remain outstanding.

Reconciliation separates earned, referral, redeemed, reversed, adjusted and legacy movements. Estimated discount kobo uses positive net balance and current approved branch discount rate; it is NOT cash, wallet balance or recognised accounting liability. Legacy branch attribution is uncertain. Margin/campaign budget conclusions require actual approved costing; no fabricated estimates are provided.

## Implemented completion rules

Policy now also supports serviceEarningEnabled/eligibleServiceIds (OFF/empty for old policies) and an initially empty offers array. Each approved offer has a stable ID, title, point cost, kobo budget, validity interval, per-customer maximum uses, frequencyDays, active and smartRefill controls. Active offers must fund at least one claim and meet the programme minimum. Both policy and technical flags are required. Budgets are per approved version/offer; customer reuse/frequency checks span versions. Immutable committed claims consume budget; refunds do not replenish it. General point discounts remain a separate approved programme benefit, not an additional offer discount.

Partial product/gas refunds use persisted fulfilled-order line quantities and frozen eligibility. Cumulative floor allocation distributes the original point-funded discount and spent points; each request receives only the increment beyond earlier approved requests. Earned-point reversal is proportional to the original posted award and its refund baseline, capped at that award. Final remaining refund closes rounding exactly, including delivery only on a full remaining-order request. Refunds never update stock, cylinder custody or physical handover. Zero-monetary, points-funded product refunds restore points without inserting a fictional wallet transaction. Independent finance approval remains mandatory. Service-only refunds retain the existing full-service refund workflow and reverse the original labour award once.

Service earning uses acknowledged completed service reports, actual covered invoice payments and frozen approved labour price; callout, materials and taxes are excluded. Referral review states held/cleared/blocked require independent owner and finance approval. Holds prevent award posting; an audited retry is available after clearance. Shared addresses are not automatically treated as fraud.

Smart Refill offers are available only with a genuine forecast from verified history and current marketing/refill consent. In-app notices use the shared Africa/Lagos quiet-hours rule, stable notice evidence and offer frequency limits. They create neither an order nor points. No external messaging provider is activated.
